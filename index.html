    • A model, not an official flood map: it raises the water the same amount along the whole reach and ignores bridges, debris and how fast the water moves.
    • Checked against NWS flood notes for this gauge: River Center Park and the Hermosa St backyards flood within a foot of 11 ft as NWS says; the Hot Springs Blvd & San Juan St lot floods about 1 ft earlier than NWS says (16.2 ft), which fits what was seen on Oct 23 2026, when it flooded at the 15.02 ft crest.
    • The ground model is from 2018 and has buildings removed, so “water at its walls” means water reaches the building, not how deep it is inside. Channel changes since 2018 aren’t captured.
    • The 1911 level was measured on an older gauge datum, so its line here is approximate.
    • Above about 18 ft these are what-ifs beyond anything recorded. Read them as the ground that sits that high above the river, not as a forecast: at those levels side creeks, bridges and the valley shape matter more than this model allows.
